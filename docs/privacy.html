1. What We Collect

The only personal information collected is the email address you provide when requesting access. We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or payment card details — payment is handled entirely through Venmo, outside this site, under Venmo's own privacy policy.

2. How We Use It

Your email address is used only to:

  • Send the daily picks email(s) for the sport(s) you're subscribed to
  • Send a receipt confirming your active access when you subscribe or renew

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party for marketing purposes.

3. How It's Stored

Subscriber email addresses are kept in a private, access-restricted repository and are used only to determine who receives which emails. Emails are sent through Gmail’s SMTP service; Google processes them as our email provider under Google’s own privacy policy. We use reasonable measures to protect this data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Data Retention

Your email stops receiving picks automatically 30 days after your last payment, unless renewed. We don't currently auto-delete the address from our records after it lapses — if you'd like it removed entirely, contact us and we'll delete it.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Like most websites, it is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive standard request information (such as your IP address and browser details) when your browser fetches those resources. We do not receive or store that information.

6. Your Rights

7. Children's Privacy
